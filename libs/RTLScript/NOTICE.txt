# RTLScript (vendored)

Source: https://github.com/oscar7070/RTLScript
License: MIT (see LICENSE)
Fork of FarsiType (https://github.com/AmyrAhmady/FarsiType)

Used by fps-overlay to reshape Arabic / Farsi / Urdu / Hebrew text for Dear ImGui
(which has no built-in RTL / OpenType shaping).

Not used: JimmyLefevre/kb (`kb_text_shape.h`) — full OpenType shaper that outputs
glyph IDs and requires a custom draw path; incompatible with ImGui::Text* without
rewriting ImGui text rendering.
